Free AI Passport Size Photo Maker Online

Welcome to the Student Help Club Passport Photo Maker. Generating professional, studio-quality passport photos has never been easier. Designed specifically for CSC operators, students, and job seekers, our AI-powered tool automatically removes messy backgrounds and replaces them with official colors.

Why Use Our Auto Passport Photo Generator?

  • 100% Free AI Background Removal: No need to use Photoshop or manual eraser tools. Our advanced AI detects the person and crops them perfectly.
  • Official Background Colors: Quickly swap to Standard Blue, Pure White, or Red backgrounds required for various government forms, visas, and university admissions.
  • Custom A4 Print Sheets: Forget the hassle of aligning photos manually for printing. Select our 6, 12, or 18 Photos Sheet options, and the tool will instantly generate a ready-to-print A4 canvas.
  • High-Definition (HD) Exports: We ensure your generated passport sheets are exported at maximum resolution (300 DPI) for crystal clear photo paper printing.

How to Create a Passport Photo in 3 Steps

You can generate a full sheet of passport photos in less than 30 seconds:

  1. Upload Your Photo: Click the upload area and select a clear, front-facing photo of yourself (JPG or PNG).
  2. Let AI Process: Verify the reCAPTCHA and click "Generate". The AI will automatically isolate your portrait and crop it to the standard 3.5cm x 4.5cm Indian passport ratio.
  3. Select Layout & Download: Choose your preferred background color, select how many photos you want on the A4 sheet (Single, 6, 12, or 18), and click Download to save your HD print file!

Data Privacy & Security

Your privacy is strictly maintained. The images processed by our AI are immediately discarded after processing and are never stored on our servers. You can confidently process personal and client photos safely.
