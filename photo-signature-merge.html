How to use the Photo & Signature Merger Tool?

Our online document optimization utility runs entirely inside your client-side browser interface, keeping your files completely private. Many government and academic forms (like SSC, UPSC, Banking) require you to upload a photo with your signature merged directly at the bottom within a strict size limit (e.g., Under 50 KB).

Step-by-Step Document Modification Guide

  • 1. Upload Photo: Select your standard passport size photograph.
  • 2. Upload Signature: Select a photo of your signature (preferably signed on blank white paper). Our AI-level script will instantly remove the white background.
  • 3. Set Target Size: Enter your required maximum file size in the KB field. The default is set to 50 KB, perfect for most forms.
  • 4. Merge & Download: Click the process button. The tool will place your transparent signature cleanly at the bottom of your photo, compress it to your exact KB, and make it ready for instant download.
