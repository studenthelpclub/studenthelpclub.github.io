Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Welcome to Student Help Club. Your privacy is highly important to us. This policy explains how we handle and process information transparently across our web application platform.

1. Data Processing

Our processing tools (including the Image Resizer, PDF Compilers, and Text Utilities) execute scripts directly inside your client-side web browser thread. We do not upload, host, store, or distribute your private files or images on our servers. Your data remains fully private, encrypted local memory, and secure on your own hardware device at all times.

2. Cookies and Ads

We work with Google AdSense to manage and display promotional layout elements cleanly. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (known as DART cookies) to serve relevant advertisements to our users based on their previous visits to our site and other sites on the internet. Users may choose to opt-out of the use of the DART cookie for personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Third-Party Links

Our ecosystem incorporates direct interface links directing to official social communication networks (such as our YouTube community channels and Telegram update hubs). We do not assume tracking liabilities or maintain authority over the privacy parameters enforced across those external platforms.

4. Contact Us

If you maintain any procedural inquiries regarding this structural Privacy Policy template, you can interface with our development team instantly through our official social support verification lines.

AI & Modern SEO Glossary

To ensure total transparency regarding our digital content and search engine practices, here is a glossary of the modern AI and SEO frameworks we use to provide you with the best utilities:

1. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

Structuring digital content to provide direct, concise, and clear answers specifically designed for AI and voice assistants (like Google Assistant, Siri, or Alexa).

2. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

Optimizing content to ensure visibility and features within AI-generated search summaries, such as Google AI Overviews and Bing Copilot.

3. LLMO (Large Language Model Optimization)

Ensuring that Large Language Models (like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude) properly understand your brand and context, allowing them to naturally recommend your resources in their natural language responses.

4. AISEO (AI Search Optimization)

A broad umbrella term covering all optimizations (including AEO, GEO, and LLMO) aimed at making content highly readable, contextual, and valuable for modern AI algorithms.

5. E-E-A-T (Google Framework)

Google's foundational quality guideline standing for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness. Creating content that meets these standards ensures higher ranking and better visibility on modern search platforms.
