About PAN Card

A Permanent Account Number (PAN) is a ten-character alphanumeric identifier issued by the Indian Income Tax Department. It is a vital document for all taxpayers and is mandatory for major financial transactions, opening a bank account, investing in mutual funds, and receiving taxable salary or professional fees.

Whether you are a student turning 18, a job seeker, or someone who needs to update their existing details, having an accurate and updated PAN card is strictly necessary.

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card Must have your correct Date of Birth and your active mobile number linked for OTP verification.
  • Passport Size Photographs Two recent, clear, and colored photographs against a light background.
  • Scanned Signature Sign using a black ink pen on a blank white paper.
  • Proof of Change (Corrections Only) E.g., 10th Marksheet or Birth Certificate for DOB changes, Marriage Certificate for Name changes.

How to Apply Online

Follow these simple steps if you are submitting the application yourself on the official portal:

1

Visit Official Portal & Register

Click the official NSDL/UTIITSL link below. Select Application Type (Form 49A for Indian Citizens) and generate your token number.

2

Fill Form & Upload Documents

Enter your personal details exactly as they appear on your Aadhaar card. Upload your resized photo, signature, and Aadhaar PDF.

3

Pay Processing Fee

Complete the payment of ₹107 (approx.) via UPI, Debit Card, or Net Banking. Download your acknowledgement slip.

4

Aadhaar e-KYC Verification

Verify your application using Aadhaar OTP. After successful verification, your E-PAN will be emailed within 3-5 days.
