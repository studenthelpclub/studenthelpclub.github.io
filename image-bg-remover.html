Why Choose Our Transparent Background Maker?

  • 100% Automatic & Fast: No more manual selecting or lasso tools. Just upload your photo, and our AI does the heavy lifting instantly.
  • High-Definition Edge Detection: Struggling with complex hair or fur? Our AI uses Alpha Matting technology to ensure smooth, professional edges without those ugly white borders.

How to Remove Background from Image Online?

You don't need any graphic design skills or Photoshop to use our tool. Just follow three simple steps:

  1. Upload: Click on the upload box or drag-and-drop your image (JPG, PNG, or mobile HEIC formats are supported).
  2. Process: Verify the reCAPTCHA and hit "Remove Background". Our cloud AI will analyze and erase the background automatically.
  3. Download: Change the background color if needed using our preset color palette, and click download to save your new transparent PNG image.
