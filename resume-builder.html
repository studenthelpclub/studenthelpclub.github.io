Why an ATS-Friendly Single-Page Resume is Crucial for Students

Most modern recruiters and corporate recruitment management boards utilize Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter out resume profiles automatedly before a manual human screening loop occurs. Complicated layouts, multi-column designs, or heavy embedded graphic tracks often crash ATS readers, leading to instant candidate rejection. This clean, single-page matrix formatting engine builds an industry-standard structural profile optimized for compliance logs.
